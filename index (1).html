Our Story

Where Style Meets Tradition

Sri Seetharam Stores & Readymades has been a trusted name in Nakkudi, serving the community with a wide range of quality clothing at honest prices. Located on SH 49, we are the go-to destination for families, men, women, and children looking for both everyday wear and special occasion readymades.

We believe great clothing should be accessible. Our curated collections blend classic styles with contemporary trends — all carefully chosen to suit every taste and budget.
